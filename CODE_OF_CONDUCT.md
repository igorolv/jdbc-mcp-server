# Code of Conduct

## Participation Standards

Project participants are expected to maintain a respectful and professional environment.

Expected behavior:

- be considerate toward other participants;
- focus on technical arguments and reproducible facts;
- accept constructive feedback in good faith;
- respect different experience levels and contexts.

Unacceptable behavior:

- insults, personal attacks, or harassment;
- discriminatory language;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- intentionally introducing malicious code, secret leaks, or unsafe instructions.

## Reporting Problems

If a participant violates these rules, report it to the project maintainer through a GitHub issue
or another available contact channel. For security-related problems, follow `SECURITY.md` and do
not publish sensitive details in an open issue.

The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or issues, restrict participation, or take
other measures needed to preserve a healthy working environment.
